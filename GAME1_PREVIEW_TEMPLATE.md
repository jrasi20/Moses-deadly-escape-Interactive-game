## PART 3: HOW TO ADD OVERLAYS IN CAPCUT (free mobile editor)

1. **Import your gameplay video** into CapCut.
2. **Add text layers:**
   - Tap `Text` → tap on your video → type your overlay text.
   - Tap the text → tap again to edit style (color, font size, shadow).
   - **For 3D effect:** Style → choose a bold font (Anton, Baloo) + add a drop shadow (black, 8px offset).
3. **Position:** Drag the text box to where you want it (top, center, bottom, right).
4. **Timing:** Tap the text clip on the timeline → drag the left/right handles to set when it appears and disappears.
5. **Captions:** Tap `Captions` (or `Subtitle`) → auto-generate from audio, or manually type them.
6. **Add sound:** If your gameplay footage is silent, add background music (CapCut has a library).
7. **Export:** Tap `Export` → choose 1080×1920 (Vertical) for Shorts/Etsy previews.
