The Three Virtues of Moses

🛡️
Courage (Scene 1 — Pharaoh's Trap)
Moses saw injustice and stepped in. Courage is doing what is right even when it costs us. Kids earn Courage by surviving each scene.
⏳
Patience (Scene 2 — Desert's Edge)
In Midian, Moses became a shepherd for forty years. The prince of Egypt learned to wait for God's time. Sometimes the bravest thing is to stand still until God speaks.
💧
Kindness (Scene 3 — The Seven Sisters)
Moses defended Jethro's daughters at the well. Kindness makes room for others when we could keep it all for ourselves. Kids earn Kindness by collecting treasure bags.
