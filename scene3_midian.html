SCENE 3 - THE WELL OF MIDIAN

The Story So Far

Moses has crossed the burning desert and arrived at last in Midian. Tired and thirsty, he rests beside an ancient well - and hears the cries of seven young shepherdesses being driven off by rude shepherds. Moses stands up for them, draws water for their flock, and does not know it yet, but God is preparing a new family for him in this peaceful land.

"Moses got up and came to their rescue and watered their flock. When the girls returned to Reuel their father..." - Exodus 2:17-18 (NIV)
