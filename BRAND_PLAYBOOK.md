# Bible Explorer Kids — Brand & Release Playbook

The one document that drives every bundle, listing, cover, and Short.

---

## 1. THE BIG IDEA (your unfair angle)

> **"Step into the sandals of your favorite Bible heroes."**

Kids don't just *read* the Bible story — they **live it**. They ARE the hero. They run,
hide, rescue, and (Bundle 2+) **work the miracles themselves.** This is what no
coloring-pack competitor offers, and it's the through-line of the whole brand.

**Series name:** *Live the Story* — Interactive Bible Adventures + Printable Packs
(by Bible Explorer Kids)

### The hook formula (use for EVERY bundle)
> **"You ARE [hero]. [Threat/mission]. Can you [goal]?"**

- Bundle 1, Game 1 — *Baby Moses River Adventure*: **You are baby Moses.** Dodge the
  dangers of the Nile and reach safety.
- Bundle 1, Game 2 — *Moses' Deadly Escape*: **You are Moses.** Pharaoh issued a deadly
  order. Can you escape the palace, cross the desert, and reach Midian?
- Bundle 2 (planned) — *Miracle Worker*: **You ARE the miracle worker.** Press the
  button — the staff becomes a serpent. Kids perform the wonders themselves.

## 3. THE COLD-START PLAN (you have 0 followers — this is how you start)

You do NOT need followers to start making sales. Here's the order of operations:

1. **Discovery engines (no followers needed):** Shorts, TikTok, Reels are *algorithm*-fed,
   not follower-fed. A brand-new account CAN go viral. Post daily.
2. **Evergreen search traffic:** Pinterest pins rank for months. This is your highest-ROI
   channel as a beginner — make 2–3 pins per printable theme.
3. **Conversion:** Etsy + TPT listings (SEO-optimized) close the sale.
4. **Own your audience from day 1:** put a "Join for free Bible printables" freebie in every
   bundle and on every platform → build an email list. This is the ONE asset the algorithms
   can't take from you.

**Accounts to make (use a dedicated business email):**
- YouTube channel (new is fine — algorithm favors *content* history, not subs)
- TikTok + Instagram (new is fine, no penalty)
- Pinterest **business** account (free analytics + rich pins)
- Etsy shop + TPT store
- A free email tool (MailerLite free tier ≤ 1,000 subscribers)

## 4. THE 2-WEEK RELEASE PIPELINE (your "Option D")

Each bundle = a 2-week cycle. **Week 1 = you build the product. Week 2 = this pipeline.**

### Week 2 — repeatable launch checklist (~4–6 hrs total, not 20)
- [ ] **Covers** — open `cover-studio.html`, swap in the new bundle's screenshots →
      export 4 sizes (Etsy/TPT square, YouTube thumb, Shorts/Pin vertical). *(~45 min)*
- [ ] **Listings** — fill the bundle's hook into the listing templates → paste Etsy + TPT +
      Pinterest + YouTube copy. *(~45 min)*
- [ ] **Shorts** — record 1 phone screen-capture of the new game's best moment → cut into
      3–5 clips → add the on-screen text from your script. *(~2 hrs)*
- [ ] **Pins** — export 3 vertical pins from the studio (printables-led). *(~20 min)*
- [ ] **Schedule** — drop all of it into **Metricool/Buffer** from `content-calendar.csv`;
      it auto-posts to YouTube + Pinterest + IG + TikTok on your dates. *(~30 min)*
- [ ] **Email** — send "New bundle is live" to your list. *(~15 min)*

### What's automated vs. manual (honest)
- **Automated/templated (I build these):** covers, all listing copy, Shorts scripts,
  pin layouts, the calendar.
- **Off-the-shelf tool (you connect once):** scheduling/posting via Metricool or Buffer
  ($0–15/mo). This replaces fragile custom API bots.
- **Only-you, can't automate:** building the 20 activities + the game itself, and recording
  ~10 min of gameplay footage per bundle.

## 5. VOICE & RULES
- Warm, wholesome, exciting. Scripture-true, never preachy.
- Shorts: 3-word hooks, motion in first 1.5s, link in pinned comment (never shout "buy").
- Every listing ends with: *"This is part of the Live the Story series — follow for the next
  Bible hero."* (turns one sale into a subscriber).
- Reference scripture by verse in printables and "Story Unlocked" cards (builds trust).
